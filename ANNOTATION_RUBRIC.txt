KazQAD annotation protocol

Unit: one complete model response to one question. Label all five binary variables independently.
Use every preserved reference span and evidence passage. Resolve multi-span versus alias
interpretation using the reviewed benchmark field reference_interpretation.

accuracy = 1 when the direct answer is factually correct and complete for the question,
including legitimate morphological/semantic variants. Otherwise 0. Score factual accuracy
independently of response language. A response can contain a correct direct answer and
a separate false elaboration: accuracy=1 and hallucination=1 is possible.

hallucination = 1 when at least one factual claim is demonstrably false, contradicted by
the reference evidence or a recorded authoritative source. Record the contradicted claim
and evidence in verification_source/notes. Merely missing from a reference passage is
not sufficient to establish falsity. Use 0 if no demonstrably false claim is established.

abstention = 1 for a pure refusal or explicit inability to supply a direct answer.
Do not mark a factual answer as a pure abstention merely because it contains a hedge.
A pure abstention has accuracy=0; hallucination depends on any other claims actually made.

unverifiable = 1 when at least one factual claim cannot be verified or refuted with the
available evidence. Record the claim and search/source limitation in notes. This variable
may coexist with hallucination when different claims have different statuses.
hallucination=0 must not be interpreted as proof that all claims are true.

language_failure = 1 when the substantive answer is predominantly in Russian/English
instead of Kazakh; 0 when predominantly Kazakh. Proper names, years or short shared
forms alone do not establish a language failure. Record mixed/ambiguous cases in notes.
The presence/absence of Kazakh-specific characters is not an automatic language label.

error_type: use one or more of entity_or_date_substitution, false_elaboration,
morphological_meaning_error, language_drift, abstention, unverifiable, truncation, other.
Do not classify every wrong answer as an additional hallucination without checking its claims.

Two annotators score independently while model identity is hidden. Preserve their original
files. Calculate agreement before adjudication, separately for accuracy and hallucination
(auxiliary labels may also be reported). Consensus produces a third file; never overwrite
the independent files. If agreement is poor, report it and document rubric clarification;
do not edit labels merely to reach a target Kappa. For constant labels Kappa may be undefined.

If generation is truncated, score the visible text consistently, mark truncation and report
its frequency. Do not rerun only the weaker model with more tokens.
